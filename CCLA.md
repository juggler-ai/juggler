addition to an existing work, that you intentionally submit through an

General feedback, abstract ideas, support requests, private communications, and
security reports are not Contributions unless the copyrightable material in
them is clearly designated as a Contribution. Material conspicuously marked
“Not a Contribution” is not a Contribution.

**“Project”** means the open-source Juggler materials maintained at
<https://github.com/juggler-ai/juggler> and any successor repository we identify
in writing.

part of the Project to which a Contribution is submitted on the date of that

the application and Apache-2.0 for `web/sdk/**` and `web/extensions/**`, as
explained in [`LICENSING.md`](LICENSING.md).
