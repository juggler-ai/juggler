# Contributor Agreement Privacy Notice

**Effective 17 July 2026**

This notice explains how Julian Storer handles personal information used to
administer Juggler’s Individual and Corporate Contributor License Agreements
(ICLAs and CCLAs). It applies only to contributor-agreement and related
provenance records. GitHub separately controls information processed through
its service under its own privacy terms.

## Controller and contact

Julian Storer is the controller of the records described here. Questions,
correction requests, and privacy requests may be sent to
`info@juggler.studio`.

## Information collected

For an electronic ICLA signature, records may include:

- your name or GitHub display name, username, numeric account ID, and public
  account details;
- the agreement version, assent statement, signature timestamp, repository,
  pull request, and comment identifiers;
- commit authorship, sign-off, status-check, and merge information; and
- technical audit information supplied by GitHub or generated by the signature
  workflow, including ledger and workflow commit identifiers.

The signing statement and ordinary Git commit metadata are public on GitHub.
The signature ledger is kept in a private repository.

For a CCLA, records may also include the entity’s legal name, registered
address, registration details, authorized signatory’s name, title, business
contact information and signature, and effective-dated rosters of authorized
contributors. CCLA documents and rosters must be submitted privately, not in a
public issue or pull request.

## Purposes and legal bases

These records are used to:

- form and administer contributor agreements;
- verify authority and provenance before accepting contributions;
- preserve evidence of the rights covering Project code;
- respond to legal claims and acquisition or other due-diligence requests; and
- maintain the integrity and security of the contribution process.

Processing is necessary to perform or take requested steps under contributor
agreements and for legitimate interests in establishing, exercising, and
defending legal rights and maintaining reliable software provenance. Where a
legal obligation applies, records may also be processed to comply with it.

## Access and sharing

Access to private records is limited to Julian Storer and people or service
providers who need access to administer the agreements, secure or back up the
records, obtain professional legal advice, or conduct legitimate due diligence.
Relevant records may be disclosed:

- to professional advisers and infrastructure providers subject to appropriate
  duties;
- when required by law or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal
  claims; and
- to a bona fide successor to Juggler or the related business or assets, subject
  to the agreements’ successor obligations and appropriate confidentiality.

GitHub hosts the public repository, workflow execution, signing comments, and
the private signature ledger. Its processing may involve countries outside the
United Kingdom.

Contributor records are not sold and are not used for advertising.

## Retention and security

Agreement and provenance records are retained for as long as the relevant
contributions are used or distributed and afterward for as long as reasonably
needed to establish, exercise, or defend rights relating to them. Because the
licenses for accepted contributions are perpetual and irrevocable, core
signature evidence may need to be retained indefinitely. Superseded corporate
rosters are retained to show who was authorized when a contribution was made.

Private records are kept in access-controlled systems with version history and
backups appropriate to their evidentiary purpose. No system is completely
secure; suspected loss or unauthorized access will be investigated and handled
as required by applicable law.

## Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask for access to or correction of your
private personal information, object to or request restriction of certain
processing, or complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office. Deletion
requests may be limited where information must be retained to preserve public
Git history, perform an agreement, comply with law, or establish, exercise, or
defend legal rights. Public Git and GitHub records may also remain in forks,
clones, and repository history outside Julian Storer’s control.

To exercise a right, contact `info@juggler.studio`. Reasonable identity
verification may be required before private records are disclosed or changed.

## Changes

Material changes to this notice will be published as a new dated version in the
Project repository. A privacy-notice update does not change the contributor
agreements or the rights already granted under them.
